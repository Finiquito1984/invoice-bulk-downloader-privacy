Privacy Policy — Invoice Bulk Downloader for Amazon

Last updated 25 November 2026.

Invoice Bulk Downloader for Amazon ("the extension") is an independent tool published by Artur Santos. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Amazon.

What the extension does

When you click the extension icon on your Amazon "Your Orders" page and press Download invoices, the extension reads your order history and invoice documents from the Amazon tab you have open, using the session you are already signed in with. It saves the invoices and a CSV summary to your computer.

Data we collect

None of your Amazon order data is collected by us. We do not operate a server for this extension's download feature. Your orders, invoices, addresses, payment details and Amazon credentials are never sent to us.

  • The extension never sees or stores your Amazon password.
  • All processing (reading pages, building the ZIP and CSV) happens locally in your browser.
  • Requests are made only to the Amazon site you are on (and, if you grant the optional permission, to Amazon's short-lived invoice files on s3.amazonaws.com/generated_invoices_v2/), only when you start a download, one at a time and with pauses between them.
  • Optional PDF recompression and merging also run entirely in your browser. Nothing is uploaded to a conversion service.

Data stored on your device

The extension stores, in your browser's local extension storage only: a monthly invoice-file counter for the free-plan limit; your plan status from the payment provider once Pro is active; and the date-range and quality preferences you last chose. You can delete this data by removing the extension.

Payments

If you buy Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay, under its own privacy policy. ExtensionPay (and its payment processor) may receive your email address and payment details to process the purchase and verify your licence. We never receive your card details. The extension only receives a yes/no paid status.

Pro costs 3.99 USD per month or 24 USD per year. There is no quarterly plan. The free tier remains 20 invoice files per month.

Contact form

The form below (and the same form in the extension Settings) sends your subject, message, and optional reply address through a form-forwarding service (Web3Forms, under that service's privacy policy) so the publisher can read your message. This page does not publish a contact email address. If you include a reply address, it is used only to answer you.
